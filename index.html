OBSBOT controls

Full body needs a still camera: turn off the OBSBOT’s AI tracking before you capture the background (use its tracking hand gesture or OBSBOT Center). The OBSBOT also responds to some hand gestures, which can move or zoom it during the Fingers demo. If your model lets you, turn gesture control off in OBSBOT Center.

Try it

Hold up one or both hands and change how many fingers are out. Turn your hand sideways, it still counts. The number only changes once it’s steady, so it doesn’t flicker.

How it works

The Hand Landmarker returns 21 points per hand. A finger is up when its tip (8, 12, 16, 20) is farther from the wrist (0) than its middle joint (6, 10, 14, 18). The thumb folds sideways, so it’s measured from the pinky base (17) instead: it’s out when its tip (4) is farther from there than its knuckle (3).

Make it yours

Edit renderFingers(). Ideas: rock-paper-scissors, a volume knob from the pinch distance, air drawing with the index tip.

Try it

Pick a filter, then turn and tilt your head. Smile, frown, raise your eyebrows, blink and wink: the mood detector reads each one. Some filters react: the glasses flash gold when you smile and the horns glow when you frown.

How it works

The Face Landmarker returns 478 points and 52 expression scores (blendshapes) from 0 to 1. Each filter is pinned to a few points, like the outer eye corners (33, 263), forehead (10) or nose tip (1), and scaled and rotated with your face. The mood detector combines scores such as mouthSmileLeft, mouthFrownLeft, browDownLeft and jawOpen.

Make it yours

Each filter is one function, like drawHorns() or drawCrown(). Copy one and anchor something new. The mood rules live in readMood().

Try it

  1. Stand back so the whole body fits, head to feet.
  2. Step out, press Capture empty background, wait for the countdown.
  3. Walk back in and pick a character. Up to two people at once.

How it works

The Pose Landmarker returns 33 body points and a mask of the person’s pixels. The mask gets filled with the saved background, then a character is drawn on the joints. If the background stops matching, for example because the camera moved, the page tells you to capture it again.

Make it yours

Each character is one function: drawOtter(), drawRobot(), drawSkeleton(), drawNeon(), drawAnime(). Change the colors at the top of the script or add your own. Keep the camera still after capturing the background.
